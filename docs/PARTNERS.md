# Partner programme

## Partners

- **Reinsurers** — portfolio-level event triage; aggregate exposure after a single event.
- **Imagery providers** — licensed high-resolution tasking; Planet, Maxar, Airbus, ICEYE, Capella.
- **Survey / adjuster firms** — ground truth and surge capacity; the human review layer.
- **NPU / edge vendors** — on-site inference appliances; co-sell the managed NPU server.

## Terms

Referral fee or revenue share, no exclusive territory, joint pilot governance. The customer owns the
imagery and the assessment; the partner does not get cross-customer data.

## Guardrails

- Partner data stays tenant-scoped.
- No cross-customer model training without written consent.
- Every assessment keeps the human signature and the audit trail.
- A partner can resell the workflow, not the customer's imagery.

## Motion

1. Co-branded stage demo for the partner's event.
2. Joint 90-day pilot with one customer.
3. Production subscription; partner handles imagery or field capacity.
4. Annual partner review: accuracy, false positives, time saved, renewal.
